## Title: **“$20 Under the Tree (Santa’s AI Slop Surprise)”**

### Music Description

This is a fast, cheeky, festive alt-rock Christmas anthem with a mischievous cabaret feel. It begins with cheerful sleigh bells, glockenspiel, and a warm choir “Fa la la” that suddenly gets interrupted by a grumpy Santa spoken line. The main groove is mid-to-up tempo, driven by punchy drums, bouncy bass, sleigh bells, handclaps, and bright accordion-style synths. The verses are theatrical and almost storytelling-like, with a low, playful Santa vocal and a mischievous backing choir. The chorus explodes into a big singalong hook, layered with jingle bells, gang vocals, and a catchy “na na na” counter-melody. For Tung Tung Tung Sahur, the music briefly becomes even more important: deeper drums, a bold oompah-brass stab, a chant-like choir, and a tiny pause before the name lands like a main event. The final chorus becomes grander, with added strings, tambourine, and a choir that makes the ridiculous premise feel strangely epic.
